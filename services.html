Services

Four systems. Valley rules.

Phoenix is not a Midwest roof. UV, monsoon, and 115° days decide what fails. We spec for that, not a brochure from somewhere else.

Shingle Tile Foam Metal

Shingle

Inspect after storms and around year 10–15. Replace when tabs curl, granules are gone, or leaks stack up.

  • South and west slopes cook first in the Valley.
  • Granule loss in the gutters is a clock, not a maybe.
  • Flashing at chimneys and pipes fails before the field.

Tile

The tile lasts. The felt under it does not. Most Valley jobs are a re-felt, not a pretty tear-off.

  • Walk it wrong and you break tile. We walk it right.
  • Underlayment is the actual roof. Tile is the armor.
  • Broken pans at hips and valleys are where water goes.

Foam

Recoat when it chalks. Inspect after monsoon, before it is a tear-off.

  • UV chalks the coating. Recoat beats a tear-off.
  • Ponding after monsoon is the tell.
  • Cuts, splits, and open flashings show up as stains inside.

Metal

It leaks at flashing and screws, not in the middle.

  • Panels are rarely the leak. Fasteners and flashings are.
  • Expansion in 115° heat walks screws loose.
  • Wall transitions and skylights are the usual path in.
