Roofing systems we install

Shingle, tile, foam, and metal. We help you choose what fits your home and the Valley climate.

Shingle

Most homes built in the last 15–20 years have shingles that are near or past typical warranty life. UV and heat in the Valley accelerate wear. We inspect for curling, granule loss, and hidden damage, then give you a clear path for repair or full replacement.

Tile

The tile itself can last decades. The underlayment beneath it is what usually fails first in Arizona heat. We lift the tile, replace the felt or underlayment, and reset it so the system performs again without a full tear-off.

Foam

Foam roofs need periodic recoating when the surface chalks or cracks. Catching it after monsoon season keeps water out and extends the life of the system. We inspect the coating and the seams so you know exactly what is needed.

Metal

Metal roofs last a long time when the flashing and fasteners are detailed correctly. Most leaks happen at penetrations and transitions, not in the field of the panels. We install and repair standing seam and other metal systems with the details that hold up in the Valley.
