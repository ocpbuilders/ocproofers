What we put on roofs

Shingle, tile, foam, and metal. We tell you which one actually fits the house and the Valley weather.

Shingle

Inspect after storms and around year 10–15. Replace when tabs curl or granules disappear. Straightforward for most Valley homes.

Tile

The tile lasts decades. The felt underneath does not. Most jobs here are a re-felt and reset, not a full tear-off.

Foam

Recoat when it chalks. Catch it after monsoon before it becomes a full replacement. Strong on low-slope sections.

Metal

Leaks show up at flashing and fasteners, not in the field. Clean lines and long life when detailed correctly.
